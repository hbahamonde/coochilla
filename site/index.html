Backline / sharing

Viewed from the audience. Left/right labels in the rider use this view.

Three direct instruments. Three vocal mics.

Both guitarists sing backing vocals. The lead singer is front-center; bass and drums are at the back and do not sing. Each guitar and the bass supplies a DI / impulse-response feed to the PA.

PA-only or optional house cabinets

The band can play without stage cabinets, using the house PA and agreed monitoring. House cabinets can also be used by prior agreement, with suitable power amplification. Cabinet simulation / IR stays on the PA feed and is bypassed on a conventional-cabinet feed; separate outputs and final routing are confirmed with the band.
